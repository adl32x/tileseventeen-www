North Star Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-27
Developer: Tile Seventeen, Japan
Contact: tileseventeen@gmail.com

North Star is a location-based game: the map around you is your real surroundings. This policy explains what data the game uses, where it goes, and what stays on your device. It covers the North Star app for Android and iOS.

The short version

  • The game uses your device's location to draw the map around you and play the game. It does not use your location in the background.
  • Your exact GPS position never leaves your device. To draw the map, the game asks our map server for map tiles around you, which tells the server roughly where you are (to within a few hundred meters).
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising in the current version.
  • Your game progress, including the "home" location you choose, is saved only on your device.
  • We don't sell your data.

What we collect and why

Location

What: Your device's location, while the game is open and you have granted location permission.

How it's used on your device: The game uses your precise location to place your character on the map and to spawn nearby monsters. This happens on your device.

What leaves your device: To draw the map, the game downloads map tiles from our map server. A tile covers a square area of roughly 150–300 meters per side, and the game requests the tiles around you. Our server therefore learns your approximate area, but not your exact GPS position. With each request our server also receives your device's IP address, which is a normal part of any internet connection.

What our server does with it: It returns map data for those tiles. The map data is OpenStreetMap data that we host ourselves, so no third-party map service ever receives your location, your IP address, or anything about you or your device.

Retention: Our server does not log requests. It keeps recently used map data in memory so it can answer faster, but that cache is keyed by map tile only: it contains no information about who requested a tile and cannot be used to identify you. Our hosting provider, Fly.io, handles network traffic (including IP addresses) to run and protect the service, as described in Fly.io's privacy statement.

Game data (stays on your device)

Your level, stats, items, currency, and the home location you set are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. We don't receive them. If your device backs up apps (for example Android backup or iCloud backup), this data may be included in your own backup, under your device account's terms — not sent to us.

Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also reset your home location in the game.

What we don't collect

In the current version, North Star has no accounts. It does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. It does not include analytics, crash reporting, or advertising, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Sharing

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with third parties for their own purposes. Our hosting provider (Fly.io) processes network traffic only to run the map service for us, as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

Planned changes: ads and analytics

We plan to add optional rewarded ads and basic analytics in a future version. Before that version ships, we will update this policy to name the providers and describe what they collect. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU/UK), we will ask for your consent before using data for personalized ads.

Your choices and rights

  • Location permission: You can deny or revoke location access in your device settings at any time. The game needs location to show the map and play, so it won't work fully without it.
  • Your data: Because we don't have accounts or keep data linked to you, there is normally nothing on our side to access or delete. If you have a question or request about your data — including under Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information, the EU/UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws — contact us at tileseventeen@gmail.com and we will respond.

Children

North Star is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Communication between the app and our map server is encrypted (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes on this page and update the effective date above. For significant changes, such as adding ads or analytics, we'll also notify you in the app or the store listing before they take effect.
